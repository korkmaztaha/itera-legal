Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Itera ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google, Apple, we collect your name, email address, and authentication identifiers to create and secure your account.
  • Reading & App Activity: Books added to your library, reading progress, page counts, quotes/highlights, reading streaks, and Pomodoro session records.
  • Device & Notification Tokens: Push notification tokens and basic device diagnostics if you opt in to reading reminders.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, synchronize, and maintain your personal reading library and progress across sessions.
  • To send optional reading reminders and streak notifications (only with your explicit permission).
  • To manage subscriptions and verify premium entitlements via Apple App Store or Google Play.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services strictly to operate the app, including Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, Open Library API (for book metadata), and store payment processors (Apple App Store / Google Play). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated library, quote, and streak records at any time directly within the app under Profile / Settings → Delete Account. Upon confirmation, your data is permanently erased from our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Itera is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: support@itera.app
